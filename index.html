History of Hip Hop

The Beginning of Hip Hop

Hip hop began in the Bronx in New York City during the 1970s. It started as a way for people in the community to express themselves through music, dancing, and art.

Early hip hop included DJs, MCs, breakdancers, and graffiti artists. These different forms of expression helped create the culture that we know as hip hop today.

The Four Elements of Hip Hop

  • DJing
  • MCing
  • Breakdancing
  • Graffiti Art

The Growth of Hip Hop

  1. Hip hop begins in the Bronx during the 1970s.
  2. Hip hop music becomes more popular during the 1980s.
  3. Rap music becomes a major part of popular culture during the 1990s.
  4. Hip hop continues to influence music and culture today.
